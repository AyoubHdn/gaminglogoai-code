**Takeaway:** the two big pSEO clusters are **not** performing equally. The **logo/games** template (led by `/logo/games/minecraft-logo-maker`, the #2 page on the whole site) engages at **79.5%** — on par with the homepage and tool hubs. The **pfp/games** template engages **~13 points lower (66.9%)**, and the **pfp/styles** template is the genuine soft spot at **28.6%** (though only 14 sessions, so directional). If pSEO investment is being weighed, the **logo template is the proven winner** and the **pfp templates are where the engagement leak is** — a product/UX signal for Codex, not an SEO-structural fix.
