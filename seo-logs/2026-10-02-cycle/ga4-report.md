**First session decline after three straight gains** (852 → 976 → 1,029 → **951**), and within-window it's still easing (35.9/day first half → 32.0 second half). But two things say this is a volume/demand dip, not a problem with the site:
1. **Engagement held at 73.1%** — no quality erosion.
2. **GSC corroborates with IMPROVING position** (16.9→13.4) and rising CTR. If this were lost rankings, position would *worsen*; instead the site ranks better on fewer searches — the signature of **seasonal demand contraction** (late-Sept/early-Oct, post-summer-gaming peak), not a ranking loss.

**The entire decline sits in the two broad head pages** — `tool/landing` (−65, driven by `/ai-profile-picture-maker` −46) and the homepage (−39). **Both pSEO clusters GREW** (logo/games +10, pfp/games +28). When demand returns, the site is better-positioned (per GSC).
